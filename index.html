Local Man Takes Matters Into Own Hands, Plans Dinner

By Our Special Correspondent, Bandra

In what sources are calling a welcome development, a local man has officially taken matters into his own hands and organised a dinner outing for Sunday evening.

Mansi, named as the guest of honour for the occasion, is expected to be picked up from New Government Colony, Bandra, at 6:45 PM, with Steam Room Café being the destination for the evening.

The scooty has reportedly been asked to sit this one out, with a rickshaw arranged for the journey instead. The pickup has been sorted, the destination has been decided and all arrangements are now in place.

Sources close to the plan confirm that dinner is taken care of. The guest of honour is advised to arrive on time and with a healthy appetite. The rest, according to sources, should take care of itself.
